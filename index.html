Room mode

A teacher hosts a room. Up to five students speak, each as one speaker card; up to 30 people per room, watchers and the projector display included. How it works:

  1. The teacher picks a room name and a Host PIN and presses Host.
  2. Students type the same room name (or open the shared link) and press Join.
  3. Whoever talks lights up. When someone talks, their card lights up on everyone’s screen and on the room display.

Only “who is talking” is shared, never audio or video. In Voice mode, each student’s own computer listens and sends just “talking” or “not talking”. No account is needed. A room is deleted after two hours with nobody in it. Headphones help on Zoom.
