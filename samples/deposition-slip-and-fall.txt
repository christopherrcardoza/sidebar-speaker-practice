BY MS. ORTEGA:

Q. Good morning. Please state your full name for the record.

A. Petra Jean Noll.

Q. Spell each name, please.

A. Petra, P-E-T-R-A. Jean, J-E-A-N. Noll, N-O-L-L.

Q. Ms. Noll, where do you work?

A. Lantern Hill Hardware, on Wicker Street in Brambleford. I've been there nine years.

Q. What is your job?

A. Assistant manager on the day shift. I open the store, I set the floor schedule, and I take customer complaints when the manager is out.

Q. Were you working on March 3rd, 2025?

A. Yes. I came in at 7:00 in the morning. My shift went to 4:00.

Q. Did anything unusual happen that morning near aisle 7?

MR. HALVORSEN: Objection, form. "Unusual" is vague.

MS. ORTEGA: I'll rephrase.

Q. Did a customer report a fall in the store that morning?

A. Yes. A woman came to the front counter a little after 9:15. She said she had slipped near the paint aisle.

Q. What did you do?

A. I walked back with her. Aisle 7 is paint and primer. There was a damp patch on the tile, about two feet across. A mop bucket was at the end of the aisle. I did not see a wet-floor sign right then.

MR. HALVORSEN: Objection, form. The question did not ask her to estimate a size.

MS. ORTEGA: She is describing what she saw. The answer can stand.

Q. How do you fix the time at about 9:15?

A. The register clock read 9:17 when she reached the counter. I wrote 9:17 a.m. on the incident form.

THE WITNESS: Do you want the form number?

MS. ORTEGA: Yes, if you remember it.

A. LH-1044. We opened a new pad in January, so the numbers were still in the one-thousands.

Q. I am showing you a two-page document. Do you recognize it?

A. Yes. That is the report I filled out. My handwriting is on page 1. The customer's statement is clipped on as page 2. I did not write her statement.

MR. HALVORSEN: Counsel, a short sidebar point before you mark that. Page 2 was written by the customer. She is not in this room. Please do not ask Ms. Noll to adopt those words.

MS. ORTEGA: Agreed. I will stay on page 1.

Q. Is that your signature at the bottom of page 1?

A. It is. I signed at 9:46 a.m. I had called the store manager, Ruth Pell. She was at the bank. She told me to finish the form and to offer the customer a chair in the office.

Q. Did the customer sit down?

A. She did. She sat about ten minutes, drank a cup of water, and said she would call her own doctor. She declined an ambulance. I put that in box 6.

Q. Were caution cones in the store that day?

A. We keep two yellow cones in the janitor closet. After she left, one cone was in the closet and one was not. I found the second cone later, folded behind a stack of drop cloths in aisle 6. I don't know who put it there.

MR. HALVORSEN: Objection, form. That last part calls for speculation, and she already said she does not know.

MS. ORTEGA: She has answered. I will move on. I am marking the report as Exhibit 4 to this deposition.

Q. Ms. Noll, box 4 on page 1 says "damp tile, no cone in aisle." Is that still accurate as to what you personally saw?

A. Yes. That is what I saw when I walked the aisle with her. The cone was not in aisle 7.

THE WITNESS: Should I initial the exhibit?

MS. ORTEGA: Please initial page 1, beside your signature.

A. All right. I've done that.

MR. HALVORSEN: Same objection to form on the last question, noted for the record.

MS. ORTEGA: Noted. I have nothing further right now.
