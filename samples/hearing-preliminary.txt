THE COURT: We are on the record in Case No. HG-26-118, the preliminary hearing in the State versus Elliot Marsh. Counsel, are you ready?

MS. RAMIREZ: The State is ready, Your Honor.

MR. DOYLE: Defense is ready.

THE COURT: Call your witness, Ms. Ramirez.

MS. RAMIREZ: The State calls Officer Idris Bellamy.

THE COURT: Officer, you remain under the oath given this morning. Please sit down.

BY MS. RAMIREZ:

Q. State your name and assignment, and spell your last name.

A. Idris Bellamy, B-E-L-L-A-M-Y. I am a patrol officer with the Harrow Glen Police Department, badge 4417. I have been there six years.

Q. Were you on duty on January 12th, 2026?

A. Yes, ma'am. Night shift, 6:00 p.m. to 6:00 a.m.

Q. Did that shift take you to a construction yard?

A. It did. At about 11:40 p.m. I was sent to Fenn Construction, 220 Old Quarry Road. The call said a compressor was missing from the yard.

MR. DOYLE: Objection. Hearsay, whatever the caller claimed was missing.

THE COURT: Overruled for a limited purpose. It explains why he went. It is not proof that the machine was stolen. Continue.

Q. What did you see at the yard?

A. The front gate was shut, but the chain on the equipment cage was hanging open. A cut link was on the ground. Inside the cage, a painted rectangle on the concrete was empty. The yard supervisor, Paul Greer, was waiting by the office.

Q. What did you do next?

A. I looked at an inventory sheet he handed me, and later that night I went to a second location. I would rather describe the machine from the photograph than from memory alone.

MR. DOYLE: Objection, and I would like to approach. If a photo is coming in, we have not seen a print. I do not want a serial number read off a phone.

THE COURT: Approach.

MS. RAMIREZ: I have a still image printed this morning. I will mark the print. The phone stays down.

THE COURT: Use the print only. Step back.

Q. Officer, I am marking this photograph as State's Exhibit 2. Do you recognize it?

A. Yes. That is the yellow tow-behind compressor I saw on a parked flatbed behind the diner on Maple Loop. The diner is about 1.2 miles from the yard. The photo shows the side panel, the model ST-90, and the serial plate.

Q. Read the serial number.

A. 44821.

Q. Did you compare that plate to anything?

A. Yes. The inventory sheet lists one ST-90, serial 44821, purchased in May of 2024. The sheet states a value of four thousand two hundred dollars. The plate matched the sheet. A scuff on the fender also matched an older inventory photo.

MR. DOYLE: Objection. He did not buy the machine. The dollar figure is a number off a sheet that is not in evidence.

THE COURT: Sustained as to value. The figure of four thousand two hundred dollars is stricken. The serial-number match may stand.

MS. RAMIREZ: Understood. The State is not proving value through this witness.

Q. Did you speak with anyone near the flatbed?

A. A man by the truck said his name was Elliot Marsh. I had him spell it. Elliot, E-L-L-I-O-T. Marsh, M-A-R-S-H. He said he was waiting for a friend. He said he did not know a compressor was on the truck.

Q. What happened after that conversation?

A. He was detained at 1:06 a.m. and booked at the station at 1:48 a.m., on a charge of theft of construction equipment. I did not question him after the booking. I finished report number 26-04418 before I went off shift.

MS. RAMIREZ: No further questions.

THE COURT: Cross-examination, Mr. Doyle.

BY MR. DOYLE:

Q. You never saw Mr. Marsh drive that flatbed, did you?

A. I did not. When I reached Maple Loop, the truck was parked and the engine was off.

Q. And you do not know who cut the chain?

A. I do not. I saw the open chain and the cut link. I did not see it happen.

Q. The diner lot has no gate. Is that right?

A. It is open all night. I wrote that in paragraph 9 of the report.

MS. RAMIREZ: Objection, asked and answered. He has already said he did not see the truck move.

THE COURT: Overruled. The question about the lot is new. The witness has answered. Anything else?

MR. DOYLE: No, Your Honor. Nothing further.

THE COURT: The witness is excused. I will hear short argument at 10:30, after the morning break.
